# Source datasets

The Community Fish Detection Dataset is compiled from the public datasets below. Every source class is mapped to "fish", "non-fish" or discarded (see [README.md](README.md) and [DATASETS.md](DATASETS.md) for details).

## Datasets included in CFDv1

[**NOAA Puget Sound Nearshore Fish 2017-2018**](https://lila.science/datasets/noaa-puget-sound-nearshore-fish)

77,739 underwater images from five nearshore mesohabitats in Puget Sound (Washington, USA), with 67,990 boxes on fish and crabs.
This dataset is released under CDLA-permissive 1.0.

[**MIT Sea Grant River Herring**](https://lila.science/datasets/mit-sea-grant-river-herring/)

~262k frames from 1,434 video clips of rivers in Massachusetts (USA), with 91,482 boxes on river herring, trout, perch, bass, eels, lamprey, sunfish and pickerel.
This dataset is released under CDLA-permissive 1.0.

[**Tasmanian Orange Roughy Stereo Image Machine Learning Dataset (TORSI)**](https://data.csiro.au/collection/64913)

1,051 deep-sea stereo images from a 2019 orange roughy survey off Tasmania (Australia), with 14,414 expert-drawn boxes on orange roughy, other fish, sea stars, urchins and benthic organisms.
This dataset is released under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0.

[**Project Natick Underwater Video**](https://github.com/Microsoft/Project_Natick_Analysis/releases/tag/annotated_data)

1,118 frames from cameras on Microsoft's Project Natick underwater datacenter off Scotland, with 1,088 boxes on fish and arrow worms.
The license is not explicitly stated.

[**Roboflow Fish Dataset**](https://public.roboflow.com/object-detection/fish)

680 images sourced from the web, with 1,582 boxes covering 26 fish categories (e.g. sharks, tunas).
This dataset is released under CC0.

[**DeepFish**](https://alzayats.github.io/DeepFish/)

~40k images from 20 tropical Australian marine habitats with classification, counting and segmentation labels; the 310 segmented images (and 310 empty ones) are used, giving 374 fish boxes.
This dataset is released under CC BY 4.0 (code is MIT).

[**Deep Vision Fish Dataset**](https://doi.org/10.21335/NMDC-551736490)

1,875 images from the Deep Vision in-trawl camera system (Norway), with 4,834 boxes and masks on pelagic fish such as herring, mackerel, blue whiting and mesopelagic fish.
This dataset is released under CC BY 4.0.

[**The Brackish Dataset**](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/aalborguniversity/brackish-dataset)

12,444 frames from 89 short videos recorded in brackish water in Denmark, with 35,565 boxes on fish, crabs, shrimp, jellyfish and starfish.
This dataset is released under CC BY-SA 4.0.

[**F4K Detection and Tracking**](https://github.com/perceivelab/f4k-detection-and-tracking)

17 ten-minute videos from Taiwanese coral reefs (Fish4Knowledge project), with fish contours from which keyframe boxes are derived.
The license is not explicitly stated.

[**FishCLEF-2015**](https://zenodo.org/records/15202605)

20,000 frames from coral reef videos (Fish4Knowledge project), with 14,000 boxes on reef fish species (e.g. Dascyllus reticulatus, Chaetodon lunulatus, Amphiprion clarkii).
The license is not explicitly stated by the original authors (the CC BY 4.0 tag on our Zenodo re-upload was not chosen by them).

[**VIAME FishTrack (FishTrack23)**](https://viame.kitware.com/#/collection/65a140e8a4c218785d408b42)

~308,000 frames from 576 videos and image sequences (NOAA SEFSC Gulf of Mexico reef surveys, NOAA PIFSC Hawaii, Ifremer Bay of Biscay, CDFW California reservoir), with ~725,000 boxes in ~19,000 fish tracks.
This dataset is released under CC BY 4.0.

[**Object detection of tropical freshwater fish in Australia (Kakadu)**](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.7250921)

44,112 frames from remote underwater videos in billabongs of Kakadu National Park (Northern Territory, Australia), with 82,904 boxes on 23 freshwater fish species.
This dataset is released under CC BY 4.0.

[**AAU Zebrafish Re-Identification Dataset**](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/aalborguniversity/aau-zebrafish-reid)

2,224 images of zebrafish in a laboratory tank, with boxes and individual IDs.
This dataset is released under CC BY 4.0.

[**FathomNet Database**](https://fathomnet.org/)

An open, expert-curated database of 100k+ ocean images (mostly deep-sea ROV imagery from MBARI and partners) with 300k+ annotations over 2,400+ concepts; all fish and non-fish concepts are used.
This dataset is released under CC BY-NC-ND 4.0, with a provision allowing use for training machine learning models for commercial, academic and government purposes.

[**Marine Detect (FishInv and MegaFauna)**](https://github.com/Orange-OpenSource/marine-detect)

Two datasets from Orange with boxes on reef fish families, sharks, rays, turtles, sea urchins, sea cucumbers, crown-of-thorns starfish and lobsters.
Licenses are mixed: most images come from Roboflow Universe datasets (CC BY 4.0) and GBIF occurrence downloads (CC BY-NC 4.0), and images from Tēnaka are copyrighted by Tēnaka.

[**Salmon Computer Vision**](https://github.com/Salmon-Computer-Vision/salmon-computer-vision)

~1.4M frames from 1,673 videos of salmon passing two counting weirs in British Columbia (Canada), with 480,058 boxes on salmon and other river fish.
This dataset is released under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0.

## Datasets added in CFDv2

[**PomerFish**](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.17432128)

14,989 frames from GoPro videos in Pomeranian rivers (Poland, 2015-2024), with 21,267 boxes on freshwater fish such as perch, grayling, brown trout, roach and rainbow trout.
This dataset is released under CC BY 4.0.

[**OBSEA**](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.14888328)

4,120 images from the OBSEA seafloor observatory (NW Mediterranean, Spain), with 34,728 fish boxes and a few on other animals, over 74 source classes.
This dataset is released under CC BY 4.0.

[**CoralscapesV2**](https://josauder.github.io/coralscapesv2/)

2,433 densely annotated frames from diver cameras at Red Sea coral reefs, with 95-class semantic segmentation and 65,738 fish instance masks.
This dataset is released under Apache-2.0.

[**Orange Chromide Pond Fish Detection**](https://doi.org/10.17632/7w45jx35hd.1)

586 underwater images from ponds in South India, with 10,607 boxes on orange chromide and other cichlids.
This dataset is released under CC BY 4.0.

[**PEBL Seaweed and Shellfish Farm Fauna**](https://lilawildlife.blob.core.windows.net/lila-wildlife/community-fish-detection-dataset/pebl_farm_fauna_v1.0.zip)

1,035 frames from fixed cameras on two UK seaweed and shellfish farms (turbid, temperate water), with 2,464 boxes on fish, crabs, jellyfish, starfish and gastropods.
This dataset is released under CDLA-permissive 1.0.

[**CoReFish (Caribbean Coral Reef Fish)**](https://warplab.github.io/corefish/)

14,580 frames from diver transect videos at 5 coral reef sites in the US Virgin Islands (2016-2023), with 100,074 expert-drawn fish boxes and tracks.
This dataset is released under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0.

[**FjordFish**](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.17950781)

3,002 images from baited and unbaited underwater video along the Norwegian and Swedish coasts, a cod mesocosm and diver photos, with 6,350 boxes on wrasses, gadids, dogfish, gobies, crabs and lobsters.
This dataset is released under CC BY 4.0.

[**Newfoundland Marine Refuge Fish Classification Dataset (N-MARINE)**](https://open.canada.ca/data/en/dataset/2ae46860-f82a-4127-bb1f-b02e36ef6a70)

23,936 frames from baited, lit cameras on crab pots at 400-500 m depth off Newfoundland (Canada), with 23,890 boxes on 8 groundfish species and whelks.
This dataset is released under the Open Government Licence - Canada.

[**WIO-ReefFish**](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21360120)

1,000 4K frames from diver video transects on coral reefs in Kenya and Tanzania, with 6,768 boxes on fish of 24 reef fish families.
This dataset is released under CC BY 4.0.

[**DIGI4ECO Underwater Fish Tracking**](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20701225)

5,849 frames from 4 videos at three European marine observatories (Adriatic Sea, NW Mediterranean, Galway Bay), with 114,207 fish boxes from manually annotated tracks.
This dataset is released under CC BY 4.0.

[**YOLO-BRUV**](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.19594599)

2,640 frames from 19 baited remote underwater videos at offshore wind farms in the Moray Firth (Scotland), with 13,070 fish boxes (mostly flatfish and haddock) and 2,674 non-fish boxes (mostly hermit crabs and starfish).
This dataset is released under CC BY 4.0.

[**Labelled Mediterranean Fish Dataset (DEEPECOMAR)**](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.7534425)

1,516 images of coastal fish in the Balearic Islands (Spain) from a fixed time-lapse camera, diver videos and a stereo video, with 17,157 fish boxes over 20 species.
This dataset is released under CC BY 4.0.

[**Seatizen Atlas (instance segmentation subset)**](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.13951614)

1,200 coral reef and lagoon images from snorkellers, divers and surface vehicles in the western Indian Ocean (Reunion, Mayotte, Europa, Mauritius), with instance polygons for fish, sea cucumbers, urchins, turtles and reef habitats.
This dataset is released under CC BY 4.0.

[**AFSC Salmon and Pollock Object Detection**](https://www.fisheries.noaa.gov/inport/item/69412)

16,922 frames from a camera inside a commercial pollock trawl in the eastern Bering Sea (2019-2020), with 84,786 boxes on walleye pollock and Pacific salmon.
This dataset is released under CC0 1.0.

[**KSO Mussel Farm BRUV**](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.14713926)

910 frames from baited remote underwater videos at a blue-mussel farm and a control site on the Swedish west coast, with 1,457 fish boxes (gobies, flatfish) and 207 crab boxes.
This dataset is released under CC BY 4.0.

[**Griffith Seagrass Fish (Luderick Seagrass)**](https://doi.org/10.1594/PANGAEA.926930)

4,280 frames from remote underwater video in seagrass meadows on the Gold Coast (Queensland, Australia), with 9,429 boxes and masks on luderick and bream.
This dataset is released under CC BY 4.0.

[**Mediterranean Fish Census (autoUC)**](https://datadryad.org/dataset/doi:10.5061/dryad.f7m0cfz6f)

13,044 frames from diver GoPro transects at 8 sites on the French Riviera, with 68,590 boxes on 40 fish taxa plus jellyfish and sea urchins.
This dataset is released under CC0 1.0.

[**EyeSea Underwater Video**](https://data.pnnl.gov/group/nodes/dataset/12978)

70,195 frames from monitoring video at three water power sites (a tidal turbine in Scotland, a dam fish ladder in Washington and a river turbine in Alaska), with 45,652 fish boxes.
The license is not explicitly stated.

[**Seaclear Marine Debris Dataset**](https://doi.org/10.4121/4f1dff25-e157-4399-a5d4-478055461689)

8,610 ROV images of marine litter on the shallow seabed in Croatia and France, with 40 debris, animal, plant and robot classes; the fish, sea urchin, sea cucumber and starfish boxes are used.
This dataset is released under CC BY 4.0.

[**Roboflow Aquarium Dataset**](https://universe.roboflow.com/brad-dwyer/aquarium-combined)

638 photos taken through the glass of tanks at two US aquariums, with boxes on fish, sharks, stingrays, jellyfish and starfish (plus penguins and puffins, which are discarded).
This dataset is released under CC BY 4.0.

[**RUOD-R (re-annotated Real-world Underwater Object Detection)**](https://github.com/RSSL-MTU/RUOD-R)

13,112 underwater photos from many sources (seabed harvesting and diving videos, aquariums, web photos), with 104,202 fish and 67,937 non-fish boxes from a dense re-annotation of the RUOD benchmark.
The license is not explicitly stated.

[**UIIS10K (Underwater Image Instance Segmentation)**](https://github.com/LiamLian0727/UIIS10K)

~10k underwater photos (reefs, open water, aquariums, deep-sea ROV video, lakes) gathered from the web and other public datasets, with instance masks in 10 classes, giving 19,937 fish and 1,399 non-fish boxes.
This dataset is released under Apache-2.0.

[**NorFisk Dataset**](https://doi.org/10.18710/H5G3K5)

12,514 crops from GoPro video at Norwegian fish farms, each with one box on a farmed salmonid or a wild saithe.
This dataset is released under CC0 1.0.

[**TrashCan 1.0**](https://doi.org/10.13020/g1gx-y834)

7,212 frames from JAMSTEC deep-sea ROV videos (mostly the Sea of Japan), with instance masks on trash, ROVs, plants and animals; the fish, eel, crab and starfish masks are used.
This dataset is free for academic teaching and research; commercial use requires permission from JAMSTEC.

[**Tassie BRUV**](https://datadryad.org/dataset/doi:10.5061/dryad.sbcc2frf7)

1,912 frames from 28 baited remote underwater videos near boat moorings in Tasmania (Australia), with 5,222 boxes on 19 reef and sand fish species.
This dataset is released under CC0 1.0.
